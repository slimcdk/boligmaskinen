About the data

Homes are approximated by address points from OpenStreetMap, which are imported from the national address register. Cells in industrial areas are left out.

Road noise comes from Miljøstyrelsen's 2022 noise mapping where it exists (Aarhus and major roads, – of the area). Elsewhere it is estimated from road type and distance.

Forests and lakes count from 5 ha. Schools are folkeskoler, friskoler and private schools from STIL. Daycare comes from Dagtilbudsregisteret. Wind turbines are those in operation from Energistyrelsen. Tax and burglaries are per municipality from Danmarks Statistik.

Colours are stretched over the points currently shown, so the map always shows the differences that matter.
